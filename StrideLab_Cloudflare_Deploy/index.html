01 / Athlete profile

Assessment protocol

Record at a stable running speed. Capture a full-body side view and, ideally, posterior and anterior views. Use high frame rate footage where possible. Review multiple consecutive strides rather than a single frame.

Measurement integrity: Automated 2D pose tracking is available below. Measurements are estimates, dependent on footage quality, and are not equivalent to laboratory motion capture.
